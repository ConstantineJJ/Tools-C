# Canonical Design Graph / Model Contract — 2026-10-05

Implemented one persistent design authority in the existing PASS0 pipeline.
`pass0.json` schema 3 embeds model_contract; components, claims, dimensions,
silhouette, hierarchy/symmetry and relationships remain the graph. Assembly and
generated-view reviews pin only its id/revision/hash. No separate model_contract.json,
duplicate identity manifest, new skill, MCP tool or provider dependency.

## Result and safeguards

- HARD INVARIANT preserves established counts, mandatory properties/markings and
  critical relationships. PROPORTION LOCK preserves existing envelopes, reviewed
  ratios and numeric silhouette anchors with declared tolerances. SOFT CANONICAL
  permits minor implementation adaptations as WARN without rewriting the baseline.
- Certainty and strength remain independent: REQUIRED/CANONICAL/SPECULATIVE for
  DESCRIPTION; CONFIRMED/INFERRED/SPECULATIVE plus explicit user REQUIRED for schema 3
  REFERENCE. Unsupported hidden geometry cannot become a hard/proportion fact.
- Trust: USER REQUIRED > ORIGINAL REFERENCE / LOCKED HERO > MODEL CONTRACT >
  GENERATED MULTIVIEW > AGENT INFERENCE. Contract extraction cannot override its sources.
- Every permitted generated panel needs current Contract + image-hash review.
  Known cropped extents fail VIEW COMPLETENESS; shortening/out-of-envelope ratios
  fail. Self-occlusion or incomparable projection returns UNKNOWN. Neither becomes
  a new shape. Failed panels are excluded, corrected/regenerated or rejected;
  a usable original/Hero permits a bounded source-only handoff.
- Counts derive from physical instance bindings, not meshes/bolts. Repeated numeric
  dimensions/ratios require measurements per instance: opposite errors cannot average
  to PASS. Assembly hierarchy, source byte hashes and pinned revisions are checked.
- Verification runs only at VIEWS, MACRO, MECHANICS, GEOMETRY clay handoff and FINAL
  checkpoints relevant to the requested task. No per-operation checks. Explicit
  revisions preserve predecessor hash/change note; changed REQUIRED clauses additionally
  need USER authority and revised verbatim brief. Stale views/bindings do not pass.
- Historical schema 1/2 still parse for audit. Modeling require_stage() now requires
  schema 3 and a reviewed Contract. Migration does not invent tolerances automatically.

## Limits and stop points

The verifier compares declared measurements/reviews; it does not read pixels or
infer real object identity from mesh names. Bind assembly entries to real evaluated
scene objects and refresh them at checkpoints/resume. Fabricated measurements or
rewriting all hashes/receipts are outside its proof. The general Blender bridge
is not globally intercepted; consumers must use the PASS0 stage gate and checkpoint
functions, as the Skills require.

Stop the dependent region/checkpoint on hard/proportion FAIL or UNKNOWN, unresolved
user/source contradictions, missing required information, stale Contract binding,
or a required hidden interface that cannot be established. Keep independent supported
work possible with explicit limits. Do not revise the Contract to make a generated
sheet pass, label plausible rear pixels CANONICAL/CONFIRMED, enlarge tolerance after
failure, or hide weak form with texture/wear. Default two targeted image repairs per
defect; then retain uncertainty and use source-only evidence or report the blocker.

Automated check counts and artifact hashes are recorded in
[validation](model-contract-workflow/validation.json) and the
[regression log](model-contract-workflow/unit-regressions.log). Local implementation;
no commit or new release publication in this turn. Unrelated dirty work is preserved.
