# Anime profile — body, proportions and outfit

Part of the [anime character skill](../SKILL.md). Ratios, budgets and destination
come from the design and project profile.

## Translating a drawing into form

Identify silhouette, skull/jaw shape, eye line, hair outline and head/body
relationship. Separate observed landmarks from perspective and graphic conventions.
Register the intended reference view; do not distort the whole model to fit an
illustration's incompatible projections. For a free design, choose one coherent
style brief rather than averaging unrelated anime references. Chibi proportions
are an explicit design choice, not a universal default.

## Proportions and blockout

Use the general [body proportion guide](../../blender-character-modeling/references/body-proportions.md)
with the **approved anime subtype** and view-specific anchors: a reference-led
comparison, not a realistic-body normalization. Measure skull, face artwork and
external hair volume separately; protect accepted eye spacing, mouth and expression
when reshaping the skull/hair shell.

Establish skull, jaw, neck, torso, limbs and primary hair/outfit masses before
details. Check whether a front correction flattens the profile or a profile
correction changes the approved face width. Keep hidden-view assumptions editable
until accepted. Choose polygon editing, curves or an authorized sculpt stage by
editability and the requested result.

For a focal character in a larger scene, prove face, pose and primary hair/body
masses in a [representative lookdev slice](../../production-lookdev-gate/SKILL.md)
before heavy background dressing.

## Body and outfit

Carry the chosen proportions consistently through neck/shoulders, torso/hips,
hands/feet and clothing. Use folds to describe garment cut and support, not to
hide an incorrect body blockout. Identify rigid ornaments versus flexible cloth.
Keep garment/body layers separate where useful for editing and deformation;
separation is not proof of pose clearance. Do not delete hidden body geometry,
apply thickness or change costume identity without task authorization.

## Checks

Whole silhouette at the intended size, reference view plus three-quarter/profile,
clothing clearance at neck, shoulders and hips, and an unaffected view after each
visible correction.
