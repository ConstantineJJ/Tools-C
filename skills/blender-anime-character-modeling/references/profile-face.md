# Anime profile — face, eyes and mouth

Part of the [anime character skill](../SKILL.md). Anime style does not justify
discarding original eye/mouth artwork or skipping the general owner's face
strategy gate.

## Facial planes

Shape forehead, cheek, jaw, chin and nose as connected volumes. A small drawn nose
does not require a flat skull or zero depth. Favor the reference's facial planes
and graphic rhythm over added realistic creases. Build planes, lids and eye
placement together so identity survives three-quarter and profile views.

## Eyes

Choose spherical/ellipsoidal eyes, a shallow eye form or a graphic surface according
to expected views and expressions. Inspect lid seating and globe intersections;
avoid a floating iris or a silhouette hole behind lashes. Place iris/pupil,
highlights and lash geometry so they stay readable at the intended distance.
Painted eye details and highlights are surface decisions; physical corneas are
conditional. Occluding hair is a deliberate front layer, not evidence that the
hidden eye has been observed.

## Lids, mouth and expressions

Model the neutral lid/mouth forms the request needs. If blink/speech is required,
reserve closure and mouth-interior space and hand planned expressions to topology,
rigging and animation. Existing shape keys preserve vertex correspondence: no
silent remesh, dissolve or join on a protected expressive face. Symmetry is a
starting aid; preserve designed asymmetry and separate it from accidental drift.

## Face artwork and dark patches

For original face/eye artwork, use
[Reference Surface Transfer](../../blender-reference-surface-transfer/SKILL.md)
after the supporting forms exist; a frontal texture cannot replace correct volume.
A dark face patch may come from geometry, custom normals, a shadow mask or the
renderer: read the [look profile](profile-look.md) and diagnose before reshaping.

## Checks

Face close-up in the reference view, three-quarter and profile; lid/eye seating,
lash silhouette and mouth corners; same camera/frame/light after a correction.
